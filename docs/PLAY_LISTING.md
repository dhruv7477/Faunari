## Full description (4000 chars max)

```
Saw a snake? Stay back, zoom in, and let Faunari help you judge the danger.

Faunari analyses a photo of a snake and gives a clear, plain-language safety verdict — plus
step-by-step first-aid guidance if it might be venomous. Everything runs on your phone: after a
one-time model download, no internet is needed, so it works in the field, on farms, and in areas
with no signal.

BUILT SAFETY-FIRST
• Faunari never calls a snake "safe". Its most reassuring answer still tells you to keep your
  distance.
• When it isn't sure, it says so — and treats the situation as dangerous.
• Tuned to catch venomous snakes first: it would rather over-warn about a harmless snake than
  stay silent about a dangerous one.

WHAT YOU GET
• A colour-coded danger verdict in seconds, in plain language — no species jargon
• Clear DO and DON'T first-aid steps for snakebite
• A quick emergency panel for when someone has been bitten
• Works fully offline after the first launch
• No account, no ads

MADE FOR INDIA
Focused on Indian snakes, including the "big four" (cobra, krait, Russell's viper, saw-scaled
viper) and the harmless species most often confused with them.

HELP IT IMPROVE
After each verdict you can tell Faunari whether it looked right. Feedback (with the photo) is
reviewed and used to make the next version safer.

IMPORTANT
Faunari is an educational safety tool, not a medical device. Its verdicts are estimates, not
expert identification. Treat every snake as potentially dangerous, never approach or handle one,
and treat any snakebite as a medical emergency — call 102 / 108 (India) immediately.
```
